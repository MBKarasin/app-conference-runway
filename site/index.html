    How dates are checked

    Why this exists. An instrument to promote collaboration, the exchange of ideas and the cultivation of future APPs.

    Coverage note. International coverage is being expanded. This is still an incomplete global calendar; suggest a missing meeting or organizer.

    Every dated entry comes from the organizer's own material: its event page, the pages under its menus, a program PDF, or a banner or flyer image. Each entry links that source and quotes its wording. The time at the top of the page shows when organizer pages were last re-read automatically. That check runs nightly; it is scheduled for 2 a.m. Eastern, but GitHub can start it a few hours late. A listing whose latest check did not confirm it carries a note such as "Date needs review"; the Filters panel's "Needs review only" shows just those.

    Entries marked Expected month are projected from a meeting's usual month because the organizer has not published dates; they are never given a day and appear only when "Show expected dates" is on in Filters. Meetings that have ended keep the date their organizer published at the time.

    This page is not listed in search engines: it travels between advanced practice providers by hand. Send the link to whoever should have it.

    Details change. Confirm on the organizer's page before registering, submitting or booking travel. Distance results use ZIP or city centroids and are approximate. Not affiliated with any organizer listed. No cookies, no tracking. City data: U.S. Census Bureau Gazetteer and GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).
