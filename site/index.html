    What am I looking at?

    What this is

    A source-traced calendar of conferences, abstract deadlines, student and DNP project venues, and celebration weeks for advanced practice providers: NPs, PAs, CRNAs, CNSs and CNMs. Every upcoming dated entry links to the organizer's own material and quotes the wording its date came from. It is global in scope and incomplete by design; its reach is measured, not assumed.

    How to move

    Four views of the same filtered records: Orbit (the landing view: twelve months with the count of each record type; the arrows move a year), List (upcoming records by month), Calendar (a month grid) and Directory (every recurring series with its history). Narrow with Discipline, Focus (one record type at a time), Scope, Location (Near City takes a ZIP code or a place such as "Portland ME") and search. Every view is a shareable link: Copy link to this view.

    Reading a record

    Color is the record type: meeting, abstract call or celebration. Badges are disciplines. Each record carries its source link and the organizer's quoted wording. A record that passed its nightly check carries no label; otherwise it says Save the date, Set by rule, Recorded when published, Expected month, or Needs review / Not re-checked. The Needs review only switch lists the last two.

    The header stamps

    Verified is the latest nightly check against the organizers' pages (green; red after 36 hours). Horizon scan is the latest search for meetings not yet listed. Curator reviewed is the last supervised manual reconciliation by the curator; the other two are automated. The Fidelity and Reliability indices beneath them are defined in the AI Handoff (§3.4).
