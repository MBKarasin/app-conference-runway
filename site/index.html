    What am I looking at?

    What this is

    A source-traced calendar of conferences, abstract deadlines, student and DNP project venues, and celebration weeks for advanced practice providers: NPs, PAs, CRNAs, CNSs and CNMs. Every upcoming dated entry links to the organizer's own material and quotes the wording its date came from. It is global in scope and incomplete by design; its reach is measured, not assumed.

    How to move

    Four views of the same filtered records: Orbit (the landing view: twelve months with the count of each record type; the arrows move a year), List (upcoming records by month), Calendar (a month grid) and Directory (every recurring series with its history). Narrow with Discipline, Focus (one record type at a time), Scope, Location (Near City takes a ZIP code or a place such as "Portland ME") and search. Every view is a shareable link: Copy link.

    Reading a record

    Color is the record type: meeting, abstract call or celebration. Badges are disciplines. Each record carries its source link and the organizer's quoted wording. A record that passed its nightly check carries no label; otherwise it says Save the date, Set by rule, Recorded when published, Expected month or Not re-checked.

    The header stamps

    Updated is when the nightly check last re-read the organizers' pages. The dot beside it is green when the latest daily update finished and published, amber while one is in progress, and red when an update is overdue.
